**Contract section 4 values with the spec B defaults fail five targets**: run-on, both drift entries, the C-S gestures and the E power-brake (no gesture spins any car; the largest gesture slip is 21 deg). See the targets table.

**What the proposal changes.** Eleven section 4 attributes and one section B attribute (table below):
- chassis: `SteerRiseSeconds` 0.12, `SteerReturnRate` 25, `YawOmega` 20;
- drift: `DriftExemptGain` 4 (section B);
- spin: `SpinSlipStart` 0.04, `SpinSlipWidth` 0.08, `SpinGripLoss` 0.85, `SpinYawAccel` 25, `SpinTierFloor` 0.8, `SpinBuildRate` 6, `SpinWindowSeconds` 0.9, `SpinBelowMph` 30.

Every one of them is needed: putting any section 4 one back fails at least one target (revert table). `DriftExemptGain` 4 only adds margin to the steer-first drift. Against the previous proposal: `DriftCapExempt` is back to the 0/1 switch at 1 (C5 moves the gain to `DriftExemptGain`), `SpinWarnLevel` is back to 0.25 (C4 keeps the warning above the hold cap), `YawUnwindScale` is back to 2 (`YawOmega` 20 unwinds fast enough) and `SpinThrustLoss` is back to 0.35 (`SpinBelowMph` 30 keeps 20 mph stabs in the spin band). `YawOmega` and `SpinBelowMph` are new.

**Withdrawn claims (R14).**
- "Holds and U-turns never spin" is now tested with ordinary input (lifted U-turns and holds, A+W pull-outs, a junction turn-in, feathered W, gamepad trigger ramps, touch) and holds only because a stab needs a lift **and** a steering flick (F2). With a plain lift-and-stab (`SpinStabNeedsFlick` 0) every lifted U-turn spins (100-130 deg, independence table).
- "Every metric within 1-2% across frame rates" is replaced by the frame-rate table: run-on now varies by at most 0.3 deg from 30 to 144 Hz (C7, C8).
- "FeelSpin above 1 does nothing": with C4 the hold cap scales with `FeelSpin`, so 1.5 and 2.5 make holds wiggle harder and spin (dial table).
- The emulation of the physics part "as written" is gone: the model now follows the after-source.

## Decisions and side effects for Oscar

1. **Weight against drift parity (`YawOmega`).** The contract's yaw spring (`YawOmega` 9) fails run-on (7.4 deg) and the steer-first drift (+12%): a car that steers for 0.25 s before the drift has not rotated into the corner yet, so its drift is wider. The proposal (fewest section 4 changes) raises `YawOmega` to 20: turn-in to 63% yaw rate takes 0.13-0.17 s instead of 0.25-0.33 (today 0.017). The **heavier alternative** keeps more weight (`YawOmega` 12, 0.17-0.23 s) but needs two more changes (`YawCapMargin` 1.5, `YawUnwindScale` 4) and `SpinYawAccel` 15 instead of 25. Both pass every target. Default chosen: the proposal.
2. **Spin strength moves with `YawOmega`.** The spin yaw decays at `YawOmega` x weight / (2 `YawDamping`) (C1). Lowering `YawOmega` later makes spins stronger and holds wiggle harder: at `YawOmega` 9 with `SpinYawAccel` 25 a gamepad ramp at 15 mph reaches 71 deg. Retune `SpinYawAccel` with it: 25 at `YawOmega` 20, 15 at 12 (both pass). At 9, 25 still passes the spin targets with that 71 deg case; 11 leaves the S flick-stab at 20 mph short (67 deg).
3. **F2 keyboard conflict.** On keyboard a lifted U-turn and a lift-and-stab are the same input. With `SpinStabNeedsFlick` 1 (default) only a lift **plus** a steering flick within 0.15 s is a stab, so every lifted U-turn stays a wiggle (14-26 deg) and every flick-stab spins. With 0, lifted U-turns spin (100-130 deg).
4. **Every held lock wiggles more than today.** Holds, U-turns and pull-outs reach 15-38 deg of slip (today 10-14) because the hold cap lets L reach 0.35 x tier. The spin warning (ShiftLight 4) never lights in these cases: the warning level is max(`SpinWarnLevel`, hold cap + 0.05) = 0.40 (C4), so `SpinWarnLevel` stays at 0.25.
5. **`FeelSpin` is steep.** Spin push scales with `FeelSpin` twice (L and `SpinYawAccel` x `FeelSpin`), and the hold cap scales with it (F7). At 0.5 no gesture spins on E, C or S; at 1.5 held locks spin on E and C (97-104 deg) and S wiggles 47 deg; at 2.5 everything spins.
6. **E and D spin too.** With `SpinTierFloor` 0.8 (needed for the E power-brake) E and D also spin on every flick-stab (106-179 deg).
7. **Touch cannot spin.** The touch throttle is one summed value, so gas and brake together give 0 and physics records no power-brake on touch; a touch power-brake is a pull-out (15-33 deg). A touch flick-stab is scaled by `SpinTouchScale` 0.5. Raise `SpinTouchScale` if touch players should spin.
8. **Start grace (F4).** After spawn or R the power-brake spins once the 3 s grace is over; after an anchored start it waits for `SpinStartGraceMph` (grace table).
9. **Drift exit speed.** Drift radius passes, but exit speed is 3-12% below today (A and S worst, -10 to -12%), outside CONTRACT 5.3's ±10% for A. `DriftAssistPlateauShare` 1 gains only 1-3 points of exit speed and takes the steer-first radius to +10%.
10. **`DriftYawExempt` 1 makes drift yaw today's**: drifts carry no yaw weight. Lower it to give drifts weight; the radius then grows (at 0 it is +5 to +17%, failing on E to B).
11. **Mini-boost exit is 14-22% slower** (speed 1.5 s after a drift release; boost x `BoostShare` and lower mid-gear thrust). Critique 21's ±10% is not met.
12. **One tank of boost from top speed gains S 16 mph (today 42)**; the endless-boost top is unchanged. Boost held from rest still gives S 0-100 in 1.60 s.
13. **`FeelGearing` still changes pace a little.** With the C6 envelope the gear steps no longer change pace, but the dial still scales the shift cut (time and depth) and the torque-curve depth (`CurveDepth` = min(`FeelGearing`, 1)): S 0-100 is 3.60 s at 0 and 4.47 s at 2 (3.90 at 1).
14. **Body run-on.** Run-on on `YawHeading` is at most 4.4 deg; the body (with the AlignOrientation lag) turns 5.6-7.5 deg after a release at 60 mph (today about 3).
15. **`FeelWeight` 0 is not fully today.** Steering, yaw, turn-in, run-on and braking are today's, but the lateral cap stays: full lock at 60 mph gives 2.3-3.7 g against 3.0-4.5 g today; the drift radius is within +5%.
16. **The as-spawned Seraph is heavier (VEH-01)** and has the least first-gear thrust over traction (9%); it is the weakest C-S car for spins (power-brake 89 deg, flick-stab at 10 mph 99 deg, at 60 Hz).
17. **Measured coasting is lossier than the model** (34 to 16 mph measured, 25.8 modelled). Probe it in Play before tuning `EngineBrakeDecel` and `RollingDecel`.

**Fix spec section C as modelled** (mirrors `physics/after`, read 2026-10-20 22:45)
- C1: `r = r_steer + r_spin`. `r_steer` is the spring (ω = `YawOmega` x weight / max(`FeelWeight`, 0.05), x `YawUnwindScale` when unwinding, x authority with the settle hysteresis, capped at 60), kinematic below `YawKinematicBelowMph`, at `FeelWeight` 0, and by w x `DriftYawExempt` in a drift. `r_spin` is pushed by `Dir` x `SpinYawAccel` x `FeelSpin` x L x (0.6 + 0.4 min(slip / 0.5, 1)) while (r_steer + r_spin) x Dir > 0 and below `SpinMaxYawRate` (gate read at each sub-step's start), and decays by min(k_s h, 1) per sub-step, k_s = max(`YawOmega` x weight x (1 - 0.85 L), 1) / (2 `YawDamping`), at least 3 / `SpinSettleSeconds` while L falls after a catch (`Caught` until L rises again). Sub-steps: clamp(ceil(dt x max(ω, k_s) x 4), 1, 16). `r_steer` and the sum are clamped to `YawRateMax`. `YawHeading` integrates the frame-average rate.
- C2: a lift (throttle from ≥ 0.3 to < 0.3) arms; keyboard/touch back to ≥ 0.9 within `SpinStabSeconds` of the lift, or an analog rise from < 0.3 to ≥ 0.9 within 0.6 / `SpinAnalogRate` s, is the throttle event. The flick event is |raw steer| reaching `SpinStabSteer` after being under min(0.5, `SpinStabSteer`), or reversing sign past it. Both within `SpinFlickSeconds`, throttle ≥ 0.9, |raw steer| ≥ `SpinStabSteer`, speed < `SpinBelowMph`: the window opens and the dump becomes max(current dump, `SpinStabDumpSlip`).
- C3: `GraceNeedsSpeed` only after an anchored start or a DriveReady restart. Scenarios start with the grace over; the grace table tests it.
- C4: hold cap min(`SpinHoldCap` x `FeelSpin`, 1); warning above max(`SpinWarnLevel`, hold cap + 0.05) while rising.
- C5: w = clamp(DriftBlend x `DriftExemptGain`, 0, 1) for the lateral lerp, the yaw-cap lerp (cap x (1 + 1.5 DriftBlend) / forward speed) and the yaw spring; one downshift at a time in the drift hold by |v_h| at or below `DownshiftBrakeLand`; drift assist from DriveThrust + (A1 x thr - DriveThrust) x `DriftAssistPlateauShare`.
- C6: `MultBase` from `GearTorqueExponent`; env(v) through the mid-band speeds (halfway between the previous gear's redline speed, 0 for first, and this gear's); mult = env + (`MultBase` - env) x `FeelGearing`. Kd and the boost share from the delivered top-gear thrust. `CurveDepth` = min(`FeelGearing`, 1); rev drop `ShiftRpmSeconds` / max(`FeelGearing`, 0.05).
- C7: ω cap 60; frame-average heading; Overdrive held above 0.98 of top-gear redline after the boost ends; `TopGearOverspeed` ≥ 1.02; `FeelWeight` ≤ 0 skips the steer limiter. The locked-gate input flags, the lock edge and the unanchor launch are not modelled (no gate, no anchoring in the model).
- C8: the frame's steer is the trapezoid average of each limiter's start and end values.
- C9: duty and steady-speed averages over `DigitalDutySeconds`; the cruise short-shift needs `CruiseThrottleMin` < duty ≤ `DigitalCruiseDutyMax`, speed within `DigitalCruiseSteadyMph` of its average, ratio ≥ `DigitalCruiseUpshift`, landing ≥ max(`DigitalCruiseLand`, `UpshiftMinLand`), no drift or spin hold, and the last throttle device not a gamepad trigger.

**Existing semantics aligned with the after-source** (R14 #4, #5, #6, #11, #12, #13, #15): grip term on the tier bonus; brake lerp clamped to [0.35 min, max]; launch revs during the power-brake hold; spin free-rev blend smoothstep(L / 0.3); `LUG_FRACTION` downshift; full-throttle upshifts not held by `LiftHoldSeconds` (which counts time on throttle); every shift needs `ShiftMinIntervalSeconds` and ≥ `StopGearMph`; engine braking in Braking too; `NormalGrip` in the capped lateral share and the spin grip loss after the curve; the yaw cap relaxation x (1 + 1.5 DriftBlend) on forward speed; the boost target has both bounds; `FeelWeight` 0 corner g and drift reported.

**What is not modelled**
- Hover, pose, wobble, slopes and collisions; pitch and roll (visual only).
- Neutral revs, the countdown and the anchored start; `ImpactYawKick`, HUD, audio, remote `GearForSpeed`.
- The unexplained coast loss.
