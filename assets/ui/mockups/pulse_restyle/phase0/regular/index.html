Pulse Racers UI: Phase 0 previews, regular class

Every screen the ten accepted mockups do not cover, drawn at 1920x1080 in Barlow (the face Roblox can load; about 22% wider than the mockups' Barlow Condensed) with only shapes Roblox can draw: rectangles, hairlines, images and text. Big numbers use Barlow Condensed because they will be image digits.

Backgrounds are blurred placeholders, not game art. Names, prices and times are example values. Where the audit did not record the live wording, the text is a draft and is listed under the frame. Sources: scripts/ui_restyle/previews/.

Changes after review 3 (2026-10-09)

  • Gradient rings and gauge, as images. Oscar: "for the map outer rings, speedo etc can these be more dynamic with colour gradients etc. like we had in previous previews, these can be images". The minimap ring now blends pink, violet and cyan round the circle with a soft glow (it was two hard pink and cyan halves); the driver-rank arc is the matching gradient ring, violet to cyan. The speed gauge is five stacked images: a plate with a faint gradient rim, ticks in three weights that turn pink in the last fifth, a speed arc that runs cyan, violet, pink along its sweep with a glow and a bright tip, and boost as the inner arc in blue to pale cyan. Each is one baked 512 px image revealed by a rotating mask, so there is no per-frame cost: r00, r13, and every frame that shows the free-roam HUD behind a panel or modal.
  • Small gradients, kept restrained. The selected tile and list-row base line runs pink to violet like the main button (r03, r09); the title mark runs pink to violet; the cash chip runs yellow to a warmer yellow. Everything else is still slate, white and small neon accents.
  • Touch controls are the current ones restyled (phone gallery, c20). Rule recorded in the style sheet: touch controls keep the Classic pictograms and positions; baked gradient images are allowed for rings, gauge and touch controls.
  • Not changed here: the desktop My Vehicles panel (r09) keeps Category and Sort; Oscar asked for the drop-downs to go from the phone panel only.

Changes after review 2 (2026-10-09)

  • Button row on the rail heading line. On every screen with a rail the back, secondary and main buttons moved down from under the stat panel to the line of the rail heading ("WING 2/4 · SHOP · OWNED"), right-aligned to the right margin: r01, r02, r03, r04b, r06, r12, r21. Screens without a rail keep the row bottom-right (r04a, r05, r08, r11).
  • One smaller size for that row. 64 px high instead of 88 (text cap 20, main and buy cap 24), used on every menu. Tried against 88 on r03: at 64 the row is the height of the heading beside it and stays 14 px above a selected tile; at 88 it stood well above the heading line. The main button keeps the gradient, the glow, larger text and a 250 px minimum width.
  • Race entry vehicle choice (r12): the "3 OF 6 VEHICLES ELIGIBLE" text and the CATEGORY and SORT drop-downs are gone. The rail is in the game's default order, rating highest first; the current game does not move ineligible vehicles to the end, so locked ones keep their place by rating.
  • Roblox's top-left buttons are drawn on every screen as a faint outline (two 44 px circles in a 208 x 58 px region). They are real pixels and do not scale with our UI, so the title block now starts 12 px below the 58 px bar, measured in real pixels, at the usual 68 px left margin. At 1920x1080 that is y 70 (was 64); at 1280x720 it is 29 design pixels lower, see the first image of r21. The race HUD position and lap blocks moved down to clear the bar (r13). State and component sheets (r14, r16b, r17, r20) are not screens and carry no placeholder.
  • Chat window placeholder (475 x 274 px under the top bar) on the free-roam frames: r00, r07, r16a, r18. Nothing of ours overlaps it. To make that true: the race-start event card moved to the right, under the action bar (r16a); the garage owner controls start below the chat (r07); the objective cards start below the chat and show only the current step in full plus the next step as one line (r18).
  • Tier colours. Each tier has its own colour on the tier badge everywhere a tier is shown: tiles, stat panel, status cluster, My Vehicles list, race entry tier buttons, results, event card. The set is the game's own (GarageUI and three other screens): E #848E91, D #69BE81, C #4ACCD3, B #5289EB, A #F4BC41, S #EC5CA8. The letter cell takes the colour with an ink letter; the number stays on a white cell (ink on a selected white tile); locked and unaffordable tiles show it dimmed. Badges are on the component sheets r20a and r20b.

Did not fit cleanly, for a decision: (1) the event card cannot sit between the chat window and the minimap, so it is on the right; the contract says the top-left slot starts below the chat. (2) Race HUD frames clear the top bar but are drawn without the chat window; the contract keeps chat on in races, where it would cover the position and lap blocks. (3) The My Vehicles side panel and the modals assume chat is hidden while they are open. (4) The chat window is drawn directly under the top bar (y 70); the Studio reading was (8, 12).

Changes after review 1 (2026-10-09)

  • Driver rank bends round the minimap. A 10 px arc just outside the map ring, from 9 o'clock to 12 o'clock, filled in cyan to the XP fraction; the rank number sits outside it at about 10 o'clock. The DRIVER RANK block beside the map is gone. See r00.
  • Button row above the rail. On every screen with a bottom rail the row (back, secondary, main) sits right-aligned directly under the stat or info panel, and the rail with its heading takes the bottom of the screen: r01, r02, r03, r04, r06, r12, r21.
  • UI pushed out to the edges. Menu side margin 100 to 68 px, HUD margin 58 to 40 px, menu bottom margin 44 to 30 px, HUD bottom margin 40 to 28 px (at 1920x1080). Top-left items keep their height so they stay below Roblox's top-bar buttons. Every frame was re-rendered.
  • My Vehicles is a side panel (r09): full height on the left, as in the current game, in the new style. While it is open the minimap, district name and rank arc are hidden.
  • New frame r00: the free-roam HUD while driving. r12 changed most: its four-column grid became a bottom rail so the button row has room.
