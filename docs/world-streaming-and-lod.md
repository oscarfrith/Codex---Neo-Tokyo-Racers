# World streaming and LOD


Current baseline and acceptance: [start here](00_START_HERE.md); outstanding checks: [open issues](06_current_known_issues.md).

Workspace.World is the scoped world. LODClient/LODRuntime/LODPolicy retain ownership; Config.World.LOD holds tuning. Lighting uses Config.World.Lighting, Assets.World.Skies and Modules.Game.World.LightingSchedule/LightingPresets. No distance, loop, placement or geometry changes. Preserve current arrows and excluded thumbnail WIP.

In Continuous mode LightingServer owns synchronized cycle/config state, compatibility stage metadata and sunrise/sunset switch signals. LightingClient is the sole environment property writer, started by ClientBase. LightingCycle computes bounded targets; LightingCycleDefinition reads Config.World.Lighting's editable attributes/folders. Existing dealership and owned-interior owners request contexts through LightingClient and release to the current city phase. Their original presets remain intact. Precedence: opted-in Studio preview, dealership, owned interior, city.

Outdoor ClockTime moves uniformly through 24 hours over ContinuousCycleDurationSeconds (720 seconds). Independent complete looks hold Day at 09:30-14:30 and ClearNight at 22:00-02:00; SevenAM/FivePM anchor at 06:00/18:00 horizons, FourAM/EightPM at 03:30/20:30 twilight. Original preset names are identities, not clock instructions. TenAM/ThreePM are absent from the new look timeline. Six editable property groups remap a common phase with bounded smoothstep curves: light fades before evening haze/glare, with mirrored morning ordering. Warm Continuous copies have red-orange atmosphere/ambient tint and 1/6-game-hour full-look holds (five seconds at 720 seconds/cycle) spanning both sides of each horizon. Warm endpoints use exposure 0.15, post brightness 0.055, contrast 0.12 and lighter Decay to lift the sky. Twilight/night glare is reduced to 1.5/0.45. Twilight copies use violet Color/Decay, with a rose-pink blend and mirrored colour timing. Original shared presets remain intact. Authored endpoints and holds are preserved except the explicit solar ray-intensity gate. Latitude stays 35 outdoors and 189 in original contexts. Numeric/colour properties update on the sole renderer's PreRender connection every rendered frame; one ActiveSky uses the common cloudless ContinuousSky with a 9-degree native sun. Atmosphere colours supply sky tint. ColorGrading and original legacy fog values retain their common baseline.

NightLamppostLightClient and WindowMaterialClient retain tag/streaming ownership, switching at configured 06:00/18:00 without fades. No tag membership or physical asset changes. Config holds ContinuousLooks milestone folders and ContinuousPresets appearance copies; valid attribute changes publish atomically as state version 3, while invalid runtime edits keep the last good configuration and report LightingCycleError. ContinuousCycleDurationSeconds, ContinuousManualClockTime, ContinuousSkyName and ContinuousRayFadeHours join CycleMode, ContinuousGeographicLatitude, SteppedGeographicLatitude, SunriseClockTime and SunsetClockTime. Continuous rays keep a common Enabled flag and keep full intensity at the calibrated horizons and fade to zero through the adjacent 0.4-hour twilight windows; original indoor effects remain unchanged. CycleMode is selected at startup: set Stepped and restart Play for the original 900-second eight-stage system. Original presets/skies and StageVisuals apply there.
