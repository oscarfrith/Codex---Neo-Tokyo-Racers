- **Budgets.** Every spec is within the per-spec streetscape budget of 60k tris, 1500 Parts and 400 kit. The combined total is 1,620 Parts, which is 120 over 1500. If the integrator treats the budget as a single streetscape budget, reduce it cheaply by setting `N_ARC=4` (about −90 Parts) or by dropping the kerb-return bollards.
- **Clones.** Clones are existing city props and are not counted by sgspec.

## Hard rules and how they are enforced

`check()` runs before each save. It drops and prints any element that:

- **overlaps a road envelope.** The envelope is the road footprint from `context.json`, from road bottom −8 up to road top +60. Elements well below a viaduct deck are allowed; this applies to the promenade and ledge under the x=0 boulevard.
- **reaches more than 12 studs (+0.75) into a parcel.**
- **is a small element that intersects a building agent's current part** (from `buildings/*/*.json`). These elements yield to the building.

Other rules:

- **Roads are never touched.** No markings on roads. Bay lines exist only on the car-meet pad.
- **Frontage strip.** Pavement runs are split wherever a building part stands in the 0–12 stud strip. They are also split where the adjoining road is not at the parcel's street level; for example, the solid Y201 embankment road along the north of P05 gets no pavement.
- **Neighbour changes.** Re-run `streetscape.py` after building agents change their specs.
- **Conflict report.** `conflicts.py` gives a 3D report against the building specs; the current output is in `preview/conflicts.txt`. One benign overlap remains: X4's bridge pier stands on the terrace paving, 2 studs deep.

## Design

### Pavements (all parcels)

- **Kerb and slab.** Each pavement is a 12-stud strip inside the parcel edge, flush with the road: a 1.5 kerb (Concrete, 206/204/198) and a 10.5 slab (Paving Slab over CeramicTiles). The pavement top is 1 above the road (102 or 202) and the kerb is +0.15 above that.
  - Row 1 paving is 150/144/138; row 2 is 170/162/154.
- **Corners** where two road-facing edges meet:
  - R=24 curved kerb made of 5 rotated segments.
  - Pink corner paving (184/132/120), taken from the dealership streets.
  - Asphalt infill at road level between the arc and the square parcel corner.
  - 2 bollards on each corner.
- **Kerb returns.** Where a road cuts the strip, for example P01's diagonal road, the run ends in a kerb return plus a bollard.
- **Street furniture.**
  - `Street Lamp Tall A` every ~72 studs at 2.8 from the kerb, with the head over the road. In Studio the lamp head sits 13 studs along the model's −Z, and the pivot is at the pole foot.
  - A tree in an 8×8 concrete planter between each pair of lamps. Row 1 uses Poplar; row 2 alternates Poplar and Bamboo.

### The Step (z 4650–4706)

- **Stair-streets.** There are 4 V stair-streets, at x = −1250, −560, 690 and 1480, built on the 11-stud Y100 ledge.
  - Each has a 16-wide tower landing at Y202 that opens through the parapet onto the terrace.
  - Two flights (Wedge, 50 rise over 80 run) with a 12-stud mid landing at Y150 lead down to the ledge on each side.
  - Sloped balustrades, tactile paving on the landings, graffiti under the upper flights, a mural on the tower face and a neon strip.
- **Ledge.** Kerb plus paving. The wall stretches between the stairs get:
  - a continuous graffiti band 40–64 tall along the lower wall (SG Graffiti A/B, widths that are multiples of 40);
  - in front of the band, repeating: shops set into the wall (Metal Shutters, lintel, neon sign), vending pairs and trios, pipe bundles with AC units up the wall, benches with planters, and laundry hanging under the parapet.
  - Lamps on the 72 rhythm point over the Y100 road.
- **Terrace (Y200).** 44 wide between the wall edge and the row-2 road.
  - Kerb and paving, a concrete parapet with a yellow rail at the wall edge, and openings at each stair head.
  - At each stair head: a market of 6 `market_stall` with backs to the parapet, vending pairs and benches.
  - Lamps and planter trees line the road side.

### Waterfront (x −914..1206, z 3916–4002)

- **Promenade bands**, from the road to the water: 12-wide pavement and kerb, a 21-wide grass verge with Poplar and Maple, a 22-wide pink paving walk, a 29-wide timber boardwalk (Plywood), then the quay coping.
- **Quay edge.** `Railings Left` clones, with `railing simple` fillers up to the pier roots.
- **Furniture.** Benches face the water. Lamps run on both the road side and the boardwalk.
- **Piers.** Two fishing piers at x = −600 and 760, each 96 long with a 40-wide T head. They have concrete piles into the water (top Y76), yellow rails, a lamp and benches. At each pier root there is a kiosk (`market_stall`) and vending machines.
- **Boulevard.** The promenade runs under the boulevard viaduct (deck at Y199–201).

### West pocket park (x −2000..−1460, z 3880–4300, Y100)

- **Grass.** 6-stud Bush Grassy columns fill exactly the free lot (no road, no parcel, Y100 ground), including the crescent under the ring-road embankment and the sea hole north of P01.
- **Paths and lawns.**
  - Pink curving paths, made as Catmull-Rom splines cut into short slabs, with a paved plaza circle around a Maple.
  - 5 round edged lawns.
- **Futsal cage.**
  - A 60×40 court at yaw −14, aligned with the curved road, with 10 `chainlink_fence_20` panels and a gate gap.
  - Goals, court lines, a graffiti banner and 2 lamp floodlights.
- **Planting and furniture.** Trees, bushes, benches, lamps and a vending pair.

### East wedge lot A (Y200 triangle between the yaw −20, −167 and −75 roads)

- **Car meet pad.** A 322×150 Asphalt New pad on the wide southern base.
  - 4 rows of 12×22 bays drawn with thin lines (0.12 thick) on the pad, a yellow spine and a start line.
  - Jersey barriers with an entrance gap, and 5 floodlight masts (60 tall, with a neon lens).
- **Lock-ups.** 13 units along the west road, doors facing into the lot.
  - Every third unit is open, with a dark bay, rolled shutter, neon, tyre stacks and a tool chest.
  - Graffiti fascias and back walls.
- **Containers.** A stepped container stack of 11 boxes at the north apex.
- **Fence and lamps.** Chain-link and lamps along the east road; lamps along the south kerb.
- **Y100 strip east of P05.** A second lock-up row faces the Y100 diagonal road, with an apron, kerb and lamps.

## Assumptions, open points and notes for the integrator

- **MaterialVariant base materials** were checked read-only in the test place:
  - Paving Slab and Textile Paving → CeramicTiles
  - Asphalt New → Asphalt
  - Metal Shutters → Metal
  - Bush Grassy → Ground
  - Plywood → Wood
  - Concrete Square Tiles → Concrete

  The specs use those base materials.
- **Tree, bush and railing clone pivots** are assumed to be at ground level; the contract says this. The lamp pivot and arm direction were read from Studio.
- **Building overlap.** Some building specs put stairs and stalls in the 0–12 strip (P11 ext stair, P12 stalls and stair tower, P05 harbour stair). The pavement yields there. When their specs change, re-run the generator.
- **Stair-streets and the bridges.** The stair-streets sit under or near bridges X4, X5 and X6 but stay far below them (bridge decks are at 330–360; the stairs top out at 202).
- **Lot A boundary.** Lot A is the only east Y200 lot developed. Lots B and C south of it are left for later.
- **Tuning.** Tuning constants are at the top of each section: `R_CORNER`, `N_ARC`, `LAMP_STEP`, `V_STAIRS`, `PIERS`.

## Tools in this folder

- `site.py`: road, ground and parcel queries (`road_hit`, `street_road`, `ground`, `in_parcel`).
  - `python site.py x0 x1 z0 z1 step` prints an ASCII site map.
- `plan.py`: top-down PIL plan of the context and the specs, used for clearance checks.
  - `python plan.py --spec streetscape_*.json --out preview/plan_all.png`
- `preview_st.py`: a copy of `common/preview.py` with readable clone proxies. Lamps are drawn as pole, arm and head; trees as trunk and canopy. The common preview draws clones as full bounding boxes, and lamps show up there as big slabs.
  - `blender -b --factory-startup -P scripts/south_grid/streetscape/preview_st.py -- --specs scripts/south_grid/streetscape/streetscape_*.json --all-specs --out ... --cams drone top:P01 street:x,y,z,tx,ty,tz`
- `conflicts.py`: 3D intersection report against the building specs.
