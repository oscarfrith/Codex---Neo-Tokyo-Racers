**Code findings for the physics part (not attributes; numbers in RESULTS.md):**
1. **Spin as a separate additive yaw state** (`r = r_steer + r_spin`). In the single state as drafted, the unwind boost fights the spin and the low-speed kinematic blend erases it, so nothing spins. Even with both patched, FeelWeight 0 disables spins.
2. **A stab must be a lift-then-stab.** The rise-time reading, which the physics part uses, makes every steer-then-W pull-out a stab.
3. **Drift parity needs four choices:** exemption as a gain; the yaw spring exempt during drift; downshifts by real speed inside the drift gear hold (otherwise S bogs in 6th, −39% exit); drift assist from `lerp(DriveThrust, A1·thr, 0.9)`. With the attributes alone, radius is +5 to +25% and exit −18 to −42%.
4. **Drag (`Kd`) and boost share from delivered, traction-limited thrust.** Otherwise S tops out at 159.6 instead of 173.6 mph at FeelGearing 0.
5. **Frame rate and dials:** integrate heading with the frame-average yaw rate, sub-step the yaw spring, and scale the steer return by FeelWeight.
