Pulse Racers UI: Phase 0 previews, regular class

Every screen the ten accepted mockups do not cover, drawn at 1920x1080 in Barlow (the face Roblox can load; about 22% wider than the mockups' Barlow Condensed) with only shapes Roblox can draw: rectangles, hairlines, images and text. Big numbers use Barlow Condensed because they will be image digits.

Backgrounds are blurred placeholders, not game art. Names, prices and times are example values. Where the audit did not record the live wording, the text is a draft and is listed under the frame. Sources: scripts/ui_restyle/previews/.

Changes after review 1 (2026-10-09)

  • Driver rank bends round the minimap. A 10 px arc just outside the map ring, from 9 o'clock to 12 o'clock, filled in cyan to the XP fraction; the rank number sits outside it at about 10 o'clock. The DRIVER RANK block beside the map is gone. See r00.
  • Button row above the rail. On every screen with a bottom rail the row (back, secondary, main) sits right-aligned directly under the stat or info panel, and the rail with its heading takes the bottom of the screen: r01, r02, r03, r04, r06, r12, r21.
  • UI pushed out to the edges. Menu side margin 100 to 68 px, HUD margin 58 to 40 px, menu bottom margin 44 to 30 px, HUD bottom margin 40 to 28 px (at 1920x1080). Top-left items keep their height so they stay below Roblox's top-bar buttons. Every frame was re-rendered.
  • My Vehicles is a side panel (r09): full height on the left, as in the current game, in the new style. While it is open the minimap, district name and rank arc are hidden.
  • New frame r00: the free-roam HUD while driving. r12 changed most: its four-column grid became a bottom rail so the button row has room.
